About the Department

The Department of Computer Science at Sha College is committed to building strong foundations in programming, data structures, and modern computing practices. With experienced faculty and industry-aligned curriculum, we prepare students for careers in software development, data analytics, and emerging technologies.

Established with a vision to nurture problem-solvers, the department blends theory with hands-on lab work, projects, and mentorship.
